Our Story

The Heart of Northport

Dos Amigos Mexican Grill is Northport's premier destination for authentic Mexican cuisine. With a casual, cozy, and hip atmosphere, we're the perfect spot for family dinners, date nights, or just a great meal with friends.

From our signature cheese dip and handcrafted margaritas to our beloved Coach Dana Bowl and California Burrito, every dish is made with bold flavors and generous portions that keep our guests coming back again and again.

We offer dine-in with an on-site bar, outdoor seating, takeout, and delivery — so you can enjoy Dos Amigos however you like.
