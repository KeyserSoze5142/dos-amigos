Getting Here

Visit Us on Highway 69 North

Dos Amigos Mexican Grill is located at 13580 Highway 69 N, Northport, AL 35475 — on Highway 69 North, a short drive from downtown Northport, Tuscaloosa and the University of Alabama campus. Look for us right on the highway.

Dine-in, takeout and delivery are all available, and we're open seven days a week. On game days and weekends we get busy — call ahead for takeout or large party orders and we'll have everything ready when you arrive.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Dos Amigos' hours?

We're open seven days a week: 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM Sunday through Thursday, and 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday. Hours may vary on holidays — call (205) 737-7344 to confirm.

When are lunch specials served?

Lunch specials are served Monday through Saturday from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM only. See the full list on our Specials page.

Where is Dos Amigos Mexican Grill located?

We're at 13580 Highway 69 N, Northport, AL 35475 — on Highway 69 North, a short drive from downtown Northport, Tuscaloosa and the University of Alabama campus.

Do you offer takeout and delivery?

Yes. Call (205) 737-7344 for takeout, or order delivery through DoorDash or Uber Eats.

Do you take reservations?

We don't take reservations — walk-ins are always welcome. If you're bringing a large group, give us a call ahead of time and we'll do our best to get you seated together.

Can Dos Amigos handle large party orders or catering?

We don't currently offer full catering service, but we're happy to accommodate large party orders for game days, office lunches, birthdays and family gatherings. Please call ahead at (205) 737-7344 so we can have everything ready on time.

Do you have a bar?

Yes — a full bar with house and premium margaritas, cocktails, imported and domestic beer, draft beer and wine. Happy hour pricing runs all day on Thursdays.

Is there outdoor seating? Is the restaurant wheelchair accessible?

Yes to both. We have outdoor seating and the restaurant is wheelchair accessible.

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes. Our kids menu includes enchiladas, quesadillas, chicken fingers, tacos, burritos and cheeseburgers, and kids eat free on Sundays with the purchase of an adult entrée (one child per adult).

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Vegetarian choices include veggie fajitas, veggie quesadillas, bean nachos, the vegetarian plate, bean burrito and cheese enchilada combos, and guacamole dip.
